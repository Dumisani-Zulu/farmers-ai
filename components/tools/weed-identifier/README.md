# Weed Identifier

An AI-powered tool for identifying and managing weeds in agricultural fields.

## Features

- **AI-Powered Analysis**: Uses Google's Gemini Vision API to analyze crop images
- **Weed Classification**: Identifies specific weed species and classifies them by type (broadleaf, grassy, sedge, or mixed)
- **Severity Assessment**: Evaluates the level of weed infestation (low, medium, high)
- **Management Recommendations**: Provides detailed prevention and treatment strategies
- **Multiple Control Methods**: Offers cultural, biological, and chemical management options

## How to Use

1. **Take or Upload Photo**: Use the camera to capture an image of the affected area or select from your gallery
2. **AI Analysis**: The AI analyzes the image to identify weed species and assess infestation levels
3. **Review Results**: Get detailed information about:
   - Weed species identification
   - Weed type classification
   - Severity assessment
   - Competitive impact on crops
   - Prevention methods
   - Treatment options (including organic alternatives)
   - Risk factors
   - Specific recommendations

## Weed Types Identified

- **Broadleaf Weeds**: Dicotyledonous plants with broad leaves
- **Grassy Weeds**: Monocotyledonous plants with narrow leaves
- **Sedge Weeds**: Triangular-stemmed plants in the Cyperaceae family
- **Mixed Infestations**: Areas with multiple weed types

## Management Strategies

### Cultural Controls
- Crop rotation
- Proper plant spacing
- Soil health management
- Timing of operations

### Biological Controls
- Beneficial insects
- Competitive crops
- Natural predators

### Chemical Controls
- Selective herbicides
- Pre-emergent applications
- Post-emergent treatments
- Integrated approaches

## Technical Requirements

- **Camera Access**: Required for taking photos
- **Internet Connection**: Needed for AI analysis
- **Google AI API**: Configured API key for Gemini Vision
- **Storage Access**: For selecting images from gallery

## Best Practices

1. **Image Quality**: Take clear, well-lit photos for better identification
2. **Multiple Angles**: Capture different angles showing weed characteristics
3. **Context**: Include surrounding crops for better analysis
4. **Timing**: Photograph weeds when they're most identifiable (flowering stage when possible)
5. **Safety**: Always follow label instructions for any chemical treatments

## Limitations

- Requires internet connection for AI analysis
- Accuracy depends on image quality and lighting
- Some weed species may be difficult to distinguish in early growth stages
- Regional variations in weed species may affect identification accuracy
